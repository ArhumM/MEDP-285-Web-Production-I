ENTRY 001: NIGHT CITY

[START OF TRANSMISSION]

"The City on the Edge of Tommorow" they called it. Some tommorow, right? Anyways, best you know as much as you can about the place and the corpos at its head, nobody wants a merc who doesn't know the place they're robbing

Night City was built on the dreams of a wealthy corpo named Richard Night, back in 1994. Wanted to create some kinda paradise, free from government interference and all the usual crap. Didn't quite work out that way. Night got himself killed, and the corporations moved in to carve up his little dream for themselves. Fast forward to 2077, and you've got yourself a city run by megacorps, crawling with gangs, fixers, and every flavor of gonk you can imagine. Arasaka and Militech throw their weight around like they own the place, and, well, they practically do. You've got the glittering towers of City Center, the neon-soaked streets of Watson, and the lawless stretches of Pacifica, each with their own problems and people looking to make a few eddies. Around here choom, money talks and chrome walks. Here's a list of corpos to keep your head outta the water for:

  • - Arasaka
  • - Militech
  • - Kang Tao
  • - Biotechnica
  • - Night Corp
  • - Trauma Team


[END OF TRANSMISSION]

ENTRY 444: THE CORPORATE WARS

[START OF TRANSMISSION]

You wanna understand Night City? Then you better understand the Corporate Wars. This city was built on blood choom. That, greed, and a whole lotta corporate money. The Corporate Wars were a series of conflicts between the world's biggest megacorporations, fought over territory, resources, influence, and control. We're talking private armies, mercenaries, assassinations, sabotage, and entire cities caught in the crossfire. Arasaka and Militech were the big players, turning corporate disputes into full-scale military operations. Governments? Mostly spectators, if they weren't already on someone's payroll.

The Fourth Corporate War was the worst of them. Arasaka and Militech went from competing interests to outright enemies, dragging the rest of the world into their little muscle contest. The fighting eventually reached Night City in 2023, where the conflict ended in a catastrophic nuclear explosion in the city center. Thousands died, the skyline was torn apart, and the old Night City was practically erased.

[END OF TRANSMISSION]

ENTRY 5: THE STREET FACTIONS

[START OF TRANSMISSION]

Night City's gangs have been around almost as long as the city itself. As corporations expanded their influence and the gap between rich and poor grew, criminal organizations began filling the spaces left behind by corrupt authorities and failing institutions. Some started as neighborhood protection groups, others as organized crime syndicates, and a few emerged from the growing underground cyberware scene.

Over the decades, these groups carved out territories and established their own businesses, ranging from weapons dealing and smuggling to gambling and illegal cyberware. The collapse of neighborhoods, corporate conflicts, and waves of poverty only strengthened their influence. By 2077, gangs like the Valentinos, Tyger Claws, Maelstrom, and 6th Street had become permanent fixtures of Night City. They may have different motives, traditions, and territories, but they all share one thing: a desire to survive and maintain control in a city that rarely plays fair. So, choom, remember this: the corporations might own the city, but the gangs own the streets.

[END OF TRANSMISSION]
