ENTRY 372: THE SOLO

[START OF TRANSMISSION]

A Solo is a professional combat specialist. A mercenary whose business is violence, whether that's putting a bullet between someone's eyes, protecting a corpo executive, or making sure a rival gang never makes it home. They're the muscle you hire when negotiations fall apart and the situation gets ugly. Now, don't mistake that for being some trigger-happy gonk with a pistol and a death wish. A proper Solo knows their trade. Weapons, tactics, threat assessment, close-quarters combat. Some are ex-military, some came up through the gangs, and others are just naturally gifted killers who've learned to make a living out of it.

And if you need an example of what a Solo can become, look no further than Morgan Blackhand. Now there's a name worth knowing. A legend among mercs, a former military operative turned freelance Solo, and one of the most respected guns-for-hire to ever walk the streets.

[END OF TRANSMISSION]

ENTRY 532: THE NETRUNNER

[START OF TRANSMISSION]

A Netrunner is the brains behind the hardware. They're the engineers, hackers, and technical specialists who know how to make technology do things it was never designed to do. Need a security system bypassed, a weapon modified, or a piece of corporate tech dismantled? They're your people. Don't confuse them with your typical computer nerd, though. When a gig doesn't need a gun, usually it's cuz it needs a netrunner.

Ther was that one girl a while ago, Lucy Kushinada. A former Arasaka-trained Netrunner and former member of Maine's Edgerunner crew, Lucy was exceptionally skilled at breaching secure networks and navigating cyberspace. She eventually turned her back on Arasaka, wanting nothing more than to escape the corporation's reach.

[END OF TRANSMISSION]

ENTRY 742: THE FIXER

[START OF TRANSMISSION]

A Fixer is the middleman of Night City. They connect mercs with clients, arrange jobs, negotiate payouts, and gather information. Need a crew, equipment, or someone who can make a problem disappear? You go through a Fixer (that's me).

Take Rogue Amendiares, for example. A former merc turned legendary Fixer, Rogue runs the Afterlife and commands one of the most extensive networks of contacts in Night City. She's been around long enough to know just about everyone worth knowing, and her reputation speaks for itself..

[END OF TRANSMISSION]
