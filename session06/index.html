The McRib is a legendary McDonalds' sandwich known for its boneless pork patty shaped like a rack of ribs, coated in a rich, tangy barbecue sauce. Served on a soft, toasted homestyle bun, every bite delivers a combination of tender meat, sauce, onions, and crisp pickles. Its messy, finger-licking goodness has made it a cult favorite among fast-food fans. Often available for a limited time, the McRib has become an iconic seasonal menu item that customers eagerly anticipate.
